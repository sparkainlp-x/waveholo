== S9. SPECTRAL IDENTITY ==
sum U_z^2 = sum_k F(k) F(-k) exp(2 i z kz(k)), and sum |U_z|^2 is
constant (Parseval). So realness(z) depends ONLY on the pair
spectrum P(k) = F(k)F(-k); paraxially kz ~ k - pi*lambda*|f|^2, so
the focus curve is |1-D Fourier transform of P over |f|^2| at
'frequency' lambda*z: autofocus is frequency estimation.
max |direct - pair-spectrum| over 101 planes: 2.22e-16  (direct 0.80 s, pair-spectrum 0.29 s)
