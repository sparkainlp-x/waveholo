# Claim policy

A claim is allowed only after every relevant gate passes: named protocol/version;
complete configuration and seeds; required metric and uncertainty; all failures
reported; valid checksums and commit identity; and no mismatch between evidence
label and wording.

## Allowed after benchmark completion

- “Under synthetic condition X, the focus metric selected a focus distance within
  Y units of ground truth in Z of N trials.”
- “Under synthetic condition X, reconstruction PSNR was A ± B.”
- “Under the locked real-data protocol, the focus metric showed a positive slope
  consistent with the previously reported trend across 6 of 100 stage positions.”
  This wording may be used only if the coordinate-sign discrepancy is resolved
  from source metadata; otherwise report the signed slope as measured.
- “The method failed under condition X.”

## Forbidden

- “WaveHolo performs accurate autofocus.”
- “WaveHolo validates holographic microscopy.”
- “WaveHolo works on real holograms.”
- “WaveHolo achieves state-of-the-art performance.”
- “WaveHolo is suitable for medical or biological use.”
- “WaveHolo is a holographic display.”

The microscope slope check does not authorize an absolute-focus, product, medical,
or biological claim. Synthetic results must be called SYNTHETIC, external
measurements REPORTED, unperformed experiments UNRUN, and aspirations TARGET.
