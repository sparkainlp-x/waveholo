# Limitations and failure modes

- Synthetic reconstruction does not establish real-world optical performance.
- The real-data result is a relative slope/trend check, not absolute-focus validation.
- Focus metrics can have multiple local extrema or a shallow, ambiguous extremum.
- Reconstruction similarity metrics do not establish physical correctness.
- Results depend on sampling, carrier frequency, noise, object structure, and reconstruction method.
- Sharp objects can leak between Fourier sidebands and produce systematic bias.
- Smooth phase objects and multi-material supports remain difficult or unresolved.
- FFT propagation is periodic and can wrap content at the boundary.
- No medical, biological, diagnostic, or production use is supported.
- The benchmark requested here is UNRUN; no robustness envelope is yet established.
